# Principles

---

## Short

> According to studies, today's average human attention span is 8.25 minutes

Each part shall take no more than 10 minutes.
- cover: 1 slide (0 min)
- content: up to 8 slides (10 min)
- review: 1 slide (0 min)

---

## Storytelling

Walking away from each part, an audience shall feel capable and excited to tell the story to their spouse / friends.
