## Overview

<flex-col>

**UP**
- By hydraulic pressure
- Using hydraulic pump
(electrical)
- Regularly maintain pressure*

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**DOWN**
- By spring

</flex-col>

<container>

Gear switch down => electrically actuate hydraulic valves => release pressure

</container>

![right-top w:400](images/landing-gear-unsafe-warning.jpg)

## Squat Switch

**LH Squat Switch**

- On ground landing gear protection

**RH squat switch**

- Stall warning heating
- Engine pre-glow
- ECU test
- TAS voice warning

![bg right:48% fit](images/squat-switch.png)

---

## Downlock

- Spring loaded
- Released by hydraulic pressure for retraction
- Gear held up hydraulically
- Green lights = gear down and locked
- Red light = gear neither down nor up

<red>

Emergency operation
- free fall (by releasing hydraulic pressure)

</red>

![bg right:40% fit](images/main-gear-down-lock-switch.png)
