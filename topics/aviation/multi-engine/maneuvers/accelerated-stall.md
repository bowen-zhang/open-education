# Accelerated Stall

---

## Purpose

Simulate stall and recovery while overshooting base-to-final turn or during crosswind turns.

## Outcome

Be able to identify stall during turns.
Be able to recover from stall during turns.

---

## Setup

1. <red>Clearing Turn</red>
1. Fuel pump: ON

<flex-col>

**Airspeed**
- 85kts

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Heading**
- N/A

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Altitude**
- Recover >3000 AGL

</flex-col>

---

<col-2>

## Entry

1. Power: 35%
1. Bank: 45°
1. Pitch up until stall

Recover upon first sign of stall. 

</col-2>
<col-2>

## Recovery

1. Pitch: DOWN <hint>(to horizon)</hint>
1. Power: MAX
1. Bank: LEVEL
1. Airspeed: V<sub>YSE</sub>

---

## Exit

1. Power: 50%
1. Resume straight & level flight
1. Fuel pump: OFF
