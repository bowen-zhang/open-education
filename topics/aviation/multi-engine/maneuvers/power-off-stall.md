# Power Off Stall

---

## Purpose

Simulate stall and recovery during landing (flare).

## Outcome

Be able to identify stall during landing.
Be able to recover from stall during landing.

---

## Setup

1. <red>Clearing Turn</red>
1. Fuel pump: ON
1. Gear: DOWN
1. Flaps: LDG

<flex-col>

**Airspeed**
- 85kts

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Heading**
- Select

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Altitude**
- Recover >1500 AGL (ASEL)
- Recover >3000 AGL (AMEL)

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Vertical Speed**
- -500fpm


</flex-col>

---

<col-2>

## Entry

1. Power: IDLE
1. Pitch: at horizon

Recover upon first sign of stall. 

</col-2>
<col-2>

## Recovery

1. Pitch: DOWN <hint>(-5°)</hint>
1. Power: MAX
1. Flaps: APP
1. Airspeed: V<sub>YSE</sub>
1. Gear: UP upon positive rate of climb
1. Flaps: UP

---

## Exit

1. Power: 50%
1. Resume straight & level flight
1. Fuel pump: OFF
