# Short-Field Takeoff / Landing

---

## Purpose

Demonstrate ability to operate aircraft to published minimum takeoff/landing distance with best climb rate for obstacle clearance.

## Outcome

Master skills to safely operate aircraft at airports with short runways

---

## Before Takeoff

1. Flaps: APP
1. Line up at BEGINNING of runway

---

## Takeoff

1. Brake: HOLD
1. Power: MAX & verify
1. Brake: Release
1. Rotate at V<sub>R</sub>=74kts
1. Climb at V<sub>X</sub>=79kts
1. Gear UP upon positive rate of climb
1. Climb at V<sub>Y</sub>=85kts upon clearing obstacle
1. Flaps UP upon reaching 500 AGL
1. Climb at V<sub>Y<sub>=92 kts

---

## Landing

1. Final: 90kts
1. Short-final: 85kts
1. Power: IDLE over aiming point
1. Touch down
1. Verbalize "simulated max braking"
1. Stick: full AFT
