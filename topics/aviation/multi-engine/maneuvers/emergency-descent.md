# Emergency Descent

---

## Purpose

Quickly bring airplane down upon emergencies, such as smoke/fire, depressurization.

## Outcome

Be able to safely loss altitude as fast as possible.

---

## Limitations

- V<sub>NE</sub>
- V<sub>LOE</sub>
- V<sub>FE</sub>

---

## Setup

<red>Clearing Turn</red>

<flex-col>

**Airspeed**
NA

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Heading**
NA

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Altitude**
- Be able to recover above 3000 AGL

</flex-col>

---

## Entry

1. Fuel pump: ON
1. Power: IDLE
1. Gear: DOWN
1. Bank: 30-45°
1. Pitch: 15-20° down

Ensure speed < V<sub>NE</sub>

---

## Recovery

1. Bank: LEVEL
1. Pitch: to horizon
1. Gear: UP
1. Power: 50%
