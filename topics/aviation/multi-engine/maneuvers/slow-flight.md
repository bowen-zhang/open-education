# Slow Flight

---

## Purpose

Demonstrate airplane behavior and handling at low speed.

## Outcome

Be able to properly maneuver airplane at low speed without stall.

---

## Setup

1. <red>Clearing Turn</red>
1. Fuel pump: ON

<flex-col>

**Airspeed**
- N/A

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Heading**
- Select

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Altitude**
- Select
- Recover >1500 AGL (ASEL)
- Recover >3000 AGL (AMEL)

</flex-col>

---

<col-2>

## Entry

1. Power: 35%
1. Gear: DOWN
1. Flaps: LDG
1. Altitude: Maintain
1. Airspeed: 70kts

If turning, use <15° bank

</col-2>
<col-2>

## Recovery

1. Power: MAX
1. Flaps: APP
1. Gear: UP
1. Flaps: UP
1. Altitude: Maintain
1. Airspeed: accelerate

---

## Exit

1. Power: 50%
1. Resume straight & level flight
1. Fuel pump: OFF
