# One Engine Inoperative

---

## During Takeoff Roll

(before 50% V<sub>MC</sub>)

1. POWER: IDLE
1. Rudder: Directional control
1. Brake: Apply

---

## During Takeoff Climb

(no lower than 400 AGL)

1. Rudder + Bank: directional control
1. Airspeed: 85kts
1. Power: MAX
1. Flaps & Gear: UP
1. Identify
1. Verify
1. Feather

---

## Inflight

1. Rudder + Bank: directional control
1. Airspeed: > 85kts
1. Power: increase as needed
1. Flaps & Gear: UP
1. Identify
1. Verify
1. Troubleshooting (if > 3000 AGL)
1. Feather & Secure
