# Power On Stall

---

## Purpose

Simulate stall and recovery during takeoff.

## Outcome

Be able to identify stall during takeoff.
Be able to recover from stall during takeoff.

---

## Setup

1. <red>Clearing Turn</red>
1. Fuel pump: ON

<flex-col>

**Airspeed**
- 85kts

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Heading**
- Select

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Altitude**
- Recover >1500 AGL (ASEL)
- Recover >3000 AGL (AMEL)

</flex-col>

---

<col-2>

## Entry

1. Power: 65%
1. Pitch: up to 20°

Recover upon first sign of stall. 

</col-2>
<col-2>

## Recovery

1. Pitch: DOWN <hint>(to horizon)</hint>
1. Power: MAX
1. Airspeed: V<sub>YSE</sub>

---

## Exit

1. Power: 50%
1. Resume straight & level flight
1. Fuel pump: OFF
