# V<sub>MC</sub> Demonstration

---

## Purpose

Demonstrate loss of directional control at worst conditions.

## Outcome

Be able to identify loss of directional control.

Be able to recover from loss of directional control.

---

## V<sub>MC</sub>

Published number is calculated with:
- Standard day @ sea level
- Gear UP
- Flaps UP
- Light weight
- Aft CG
- Takeoff attitude
- Windmilling propeller

---

## Scenario

<br/><center><i>

Simulate initial climb from takeoff.
Then critical engine fails.
And pilot let airspeed degrade too much.

</i></center>

---

## Setup

<red>Clearing Turn</red>

<flex-col>

**Airspeed**
- V<sub>YSE</sub> + 10kts

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Heading**
- Select

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Altitude**
- Be able to recover above 3000 AGL
- Not too high

</flex-col>

---

## Entry

1. Fuel pump: ON
1. Critical engine: IDLE
1. Non-critical engine: MAX <hint>(92%)</hint>
1. Maintain heading (bank < 5°)

Slow down to V<sub>MC</sub> by pitching up <hint>(to ~7.5°)</hint> slowly (- 1kts / sec)
Recover at first sign of loss of directional control OR stall

---

## Recovery

1. Pitch: REDUCE <hint>(~5° down)<hint>
1. Power: REDUCE

Upon accelerating to V<sub>YSE</sub>:
1. Non-critical engine: MAX <hint>(92%)</hint>
1. Pitch up
1. Climb at V<sub>YSE</sub>

---

## Exit

1. Both engines: 50%
1. Resume level flight
1. Fuel pump: OFF
