## Simulated Emergencies

<red>

Pulling circuit breaker is NOT recommended

</red>
<br/>

<flex-col>

**During takeoff roll**
- Use mixture control
- < 50% V<sub>MC</sub>

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**During takeoff climb**
- \> 400 AGL

</flex-col>
<flex-col>

**Inflight**
- Smooth throttle reduction

</flex-col> 

---

For engines with dynamic crankshaft counterweights:
- Avoid high rpm + low manifold pressure
- Overboosting
- Prop feathering

---

## Simulate Zero Thrust

- Say "I have right engine; you have left"
- Say "I have set zero thrust and right engine is simulated feathered"

Instructor cares for the "failed" engine.
- Cowl flaps: CLOSED
- Mixture: LEAN
- Occasional clearing of engine
