# Drag Demonstration

---

## Purpose

<left>

Demonstrate performance impact of: 
- airspeed
- landing gear
- flaps

during single engine operation.

</left>
<right>

**Drag Table**

|Configuration|Vertical Speed|
|--|--|
|>V<sub>YSE</sub>||
|<V<sub>YSE</sub>||
|Gear DOWN||
|Flaps DOWN||
|Gear & Flaps DOWN||
|Windmilling propeller||


</right>

---

## Entry Procedure

**Establish single-engine straight & level flight:**

1. Slow down to V<sub>YSE</sub>
1. Critical engine: 20% (simulate feathering)
1. Maintain V<sub>YSE</sub>, altitude and heading

---

## Demo

Without touching throttle of operative engine...

1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub> + 5kts
1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub> - 5kts
1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub>, gear DOWN
1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub>, gear UP, Flaps APP
1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub>, gear UP, Flaps LDG
1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub>, gear DOWN, Flaps LDG
1. Fly V<sub>YSE</sub>, gear DOWN, Flaps LDG, critigal engine IDLE

Note vertical speed.

---

## Recovery Procedure

1. Flaps UP
1. Gear UP
1. Critical engine: restore
