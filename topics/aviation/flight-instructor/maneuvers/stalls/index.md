## Accelerated Stall

---

## Procedure

<col-2>

**Trigger**
1. Bank: 45°
1. < V<sub>A</sub>
1. Increase back pressure on elevator
1. Stall warning

</col-2>
<col-2>

**Recover**
1. Reduce AoA
1. Wing level
1. Add power (as needed)
