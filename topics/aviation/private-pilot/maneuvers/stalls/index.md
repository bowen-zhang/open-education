---

## Power-Off Stall

simulates stall during landing

---

## Procedure

<col-2>

**Trigger**
1. Landing configuration
1. -500fpm @ V<sub>Ref</sub>, trimmed
1. Increase AoA (and reduce power)
1. Stall warning

</col-2>
<col-2>

**Recover**
1. Reduce AoA
1. Wing level (with rudder)
1. Add power (as needed)
1. Flaps: APP
1. Positive rate of climb
1. Flaps & Landing Gears: UP
1. Accelerate to V<sub>X</sub>/V<sub>Y</sub>, trim

</col-2>

---

## Power-On Stall

simulates stall during takeoff

---

## Procedure

<col-2>

**Trigger**
1. Takeoff configuration
1. V<sub>R</sub>, trimmed
1. Add power (65%)
1. Increase AoA
1. Stall warning

</col-2>
<col-2>

**Recover**
1. Reduce AoA
1. Wing level (with rudder)
1. Add power (as needed)
1. Positive rate of climb
1. Flaps & Landing Gears: UP
1. Accelerate to V<sub>X</sub>/V<sub>Y</sub>, trim

</col-2>
