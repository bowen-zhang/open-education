## Why is it important?

It provides:
- Current heading

- stable reading during maneuvers / accelerations
- not susceptible to interference
- almost no error

## Errors

- Low vacuum => Drift
- Friction => Precession => Drift
- Earth rotation => 15°/h (max)

Adjust: every 15min
