## FAA Written Exam

**FAA Requirements**
- Complete ALL ground lessons
- Receive logbook entries and endorsement from flight instrcutor

**To Receive Endorsement From Me**
- Complete 3 sample tests with scores > 90
- Review incorrect questions with me
