## SIGMET (Significant Meteorological Information)

Unscheduled, valid for 4 hours, to all aircraft

<left>

- Severe icing<sup>[1]</sup>
- Severe turbulence<sup>[1]</sup>
- Severe CAT<sup>[1]</sup>
- dust storm<sup>[2]</sup>
- sand storm<sup>[2]</sup>
- volcanic ash

<footnote>

[1] not associated with thunderstorm

[2] surface or inflight visibility < 3sm

</footnote>

</left>
<right>

![w:600](images/sigmet.png)

</right>

## AIRMET (Airman's Meteorological Information)

every 6 hours, to all aircraft, mostly light aircraft

<left>

- Moderate icing
- Moderate turbulence
- surface wind >30kts
- IFR
- mountain obscuration 

</left>
