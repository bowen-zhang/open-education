## Load Factor

$$Load Factor = \frac{Lift}{Weight}$$

<br/>

|Category|Load Factor Limit|
|--|--|
|Normal|-1.52 ~ 3.8G|
|Utility|-1.76 ~ 4.4G|
|Acrobatic|-3.0 ~ 6.0G|
