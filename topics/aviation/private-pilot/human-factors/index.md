## Hypoxia

Lack of oxygen
- Hypoxic Hypoxia: <non-key>no oxygen available</non-key>
- Hypemic Hypoxia: <non-key>transportation failure by blood</non-key>
- Stagnant Hypoxia: <non-key>no blood flowing</non-key>
- Histotoxic Hypoxia: <non-key>inability of cells to use oxygen</non-key>

## Hyperventilation

Lack of carbon dioxide

<col-2>

**Symptoms**
- Visual impairment
- Unconsciousness
- Dizzy
- Tingling
- Hot/cold
- Muscle spasm

</col-2>
<col-2>

**Treatment**
- Breath normally
- Breath into a paper bag
- Talking aloud

</col-2>

## Carbon Monoxide

- Colorless, odorless
- Attach to blood 200x easier than oxygen

Mitigation:
- Turn off heater
- Turn off defrost vents
- Open air vents

## Fatique

<red>Avoid flying…...</red>
- Without a full night's rest
- After working excessive hours
- After an exhausting/stressful day

Improve:
- Exercise/strength
- Drink/eat
- Sleep
- Leverage resources
<non-key>Automation, co-pilot, controllers</non-key>

## Decompression Sickness

**Symptoms**
- Joint pain

For flight > 8000 MSL:
- No flying in 24h

For flight < 8000 MSL:
- No flying in 12h after controlled ascent
- No flying in 24h after non-controlled ascent

## Drugs

any medication that affects body in any way to safety
- Stimulants: excite central nervous system
- Depressants: lower blood pressure, reduce mental processing, reduce reaction responses


<red>

Violation (FAR 14 Part 61.15)
- Denial of application for up to 1 year
- Suspension/revocation of certificate/rating

</red>

---

## Alcohol

No flying if:
- Alcohol in blood >=0.04%
- <8h since drinking
- Intoxicated person onboard

<red>

Violation (FAR 14 Part 61.15)
- Denial of application for up to 1 year
- Suspension/revocation of certificate/rating

</red>
