## Oil Instruments

<left>

Oil Pressure Gauge

<key>

- High: clogged oil line, thick (cold) oil
- Low: bad pump, low quantity / oil leak

</key>


Oil Temperature Gauge

- <key>High: clogged oil line, low oil quantity,
blocked oil cooler</key>
- Low: improper oil viscosity

</left>
