# Operation Limitations

---

## Temperature Limitations

**Airplane Temperature**
- < -20°C: preheat engine & cockpit
- < -30°C: preheat battery
- < -40°C: NO OPERATION

**OAT Temperature at Takeoff**
- \> 20°C: remove fuel cooler inlet baffle
- \> 15°C: remove rear ventilation inlet baffle
- \> 0°C: remove coolant radiator inlet baffle
- < -30°C: install coolant radiator inlet baffle

---

## Altitude Limitations

- Max operating altitude: 16,400ft (pressure altitude)
